ℹ About ProMeet

Smart scheduling for modern teams.

Our Story

ProMeet is a simple and professional meeting scheduling platform designed to help students, businesses, and organizations manage their time effectively. We created this project to solve the common challenges of missed appointments, scheduling conflicts, and disorganized planning.

With ProMeet, users can schedule meetings, organize participants, manage daily activities, and keep everything in one place through an easy-to-use dashboard.

✨ Features

  • Easy meeting scheduling
  • Today's meeting tracker
  • Team member management
  • Calendar organization
  • Responsive mobile dashboard
  • Local storage data saving

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make meeting organization simple, reliable, and accessible for everyone by providing an elegant and efficient scheduling experience.

⚡ How It Works

  1. Open the Dashboard.
  2. Create a new meeting.
  3. Add the date, time, and participants.
  4. View today's meetings instantly.
  5. Mark meetings as completed.
